# BouquetNote message examples

These are starting points, not scripts. Copy one into BouquetNote, then replace the general details with a memory, habit, joke, or hope that could only come from you.

## Birthday

### 1. Warm and reflective

```text
Happy birthday. I keep thinking about how much you handled this year with patience, humor, and a quiet kind of courage. I hope the year ahead gives you more time for the things that make you feel alive, and plenty of ordinary days that turn out better than expected.
```

### 2. Bright and playful

```text
Happy birthday to the person who can turn a quick plan into a story we tell for years. I hope today brings excellent food, unreasonable amounts of cake, and at least one surprise you would never have planned for yourself. You deserve all of it.
```

## Anniversary

### 1. Tender and specific

```text
Happy anniversary. I still think about the ordinary evening when we talked until we forgot to check the time. I love the life we keep building in those small hours: the shared jokes, the familiar routines, and the way you make home feel like a person.
```

### 2. Looking ahead

```text
Another year of us. I am grateful for every version of the future we have imagined, and for the everyday one we are actually living. Here is to more slow Sundays, more places we have not seen yet, and more reasons to choose each other in the middle of normal life.
```

## Thank-you

### 1. For showing up

```text
Thank you for checking in when I had stopped knowing how to ask for help. Your time, attention, and calm made a difficult week feel manageable. I noticed what you did, it mattered, and I am carrying that kindness with me.
```

### 2. For practical help

```text
Thank you for taking care of that detail when I had too much on my plate to see it clearly. You gave me room to breathe and made the next step possible. I am genuinely grateful that you chose to show up in exactly the way I needed.
```

## Mother’s Day

### 1. Quiet appreciation

```text
Happy Mother’s Day. The older I get, the more I understand how many small acts of care helped shape my life. Thank you for the patience, steadiness, and warmth you gave me long before I knew how to name them. I love you, and I hope today gives some of that care back to you.
```

### 2. A shared memory

```text
Happy Mother’s Day. I was thinking about all the ordinary moments you made feel special: the familiar meals, the conversations in the car, and the way you always noticed when something was wrong. Thank you for being the person I can come back to, in big moments and small ones.
```

## Love letter

### 1. One remembered moment

```text
I keep thinking about the evening when we were both tired, talked about everything and nothing, and somehow ended up laughing in the kitchen. I love that our life is made of moments like that. You make the ordinary feel worth remembering, and I am so grateful I get to share it with you.
```

### 2. The life ahead

```text
You probably do not realize how much it means when you listen all the way to the end, remember the small things, and make space for me to be exactly myself. I want more slow mornings, unfinished conversations, and new places with you. Whatever comes next, I am glad it is ours to discover together.
```

## Letter to a friend

### 1. A gentle check-in

```text
You came to mind today, so I wanted to say hello properly instead of sending another quick message. I hope you are finding a little room for yourself in the middle of everything. I still laugh about our last ridiculous plan, and I am always here for the next conversation, whenever you have time.
```

### 2. Celebrating a friend

```text
I am proud of the way you keep moving toward the life you want, even when the progress is hard to see from the inside. I hope you pause long enough to notice how far you have come. I am cheering for you from here, and I cannot wait to hear about the next chapter.
```
